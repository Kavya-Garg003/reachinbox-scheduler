Privacy Policy

ReachInbox Scheduler is a student assignment project that schedules test emails.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We store them only to identify your account. We also store the emails you schedule (recipients, subject, body) so the scheduler can send them.

How we use it

Data is used only to run the app. We do not sell or share it with third parties. Emails are delivered to a fake test SMTP service (Ethereal).
